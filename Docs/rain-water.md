# Rain water in your room

Some rooms can have rain barrels put on their roof, and a plumbed sink in the
room then draws water from them. It takes two steps: **craft a kit**, then
**install it from inside your room**. Neither step happens in the build menu.

## 1. Get a Rain Reservoir Kit

Craft it in the crafting window, under **Carpentry**. You need:

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | **Woodwork 5** and **Mechanics 2** |
| Tool | a hammer (kept) |
| Materials | 8 planks, 8 nails, 2 tarps |

**If you cannot find the recipe, check your skills first.** Until you have
both Woodwork 5 and Mechanics 2, the crafting window may not list it at all,
depending on its filters, so it can look as though the recipe does not exist.
Searching the crafting window for "Rain Reservoir" is the quickest way to
check.

The kit can also turn up, rarely, in carpentry and farming crates.

## 2. Install it from inside your room

1. Carry the kit and go into your room.
2. **Right click the floor** inside the room.
3. Choose **Install rain reservoir**.

The barrels go up on the roof and the kit is used up. You will not see them
from inside the room; they are above you.

The option only appears while you are **holding a kit and standing inside
one of the rooms**. Right clicking the vehicle, or the room from outside,
shows nothing.

If the option is there but greyed out, hover over it for the reason:

- **This room already collects rain.** Many rooms come with barrels already
  on the roof: the campers and caravans, the ambulance bays and several of
  the fitted rooms. You do not need a kit for those.
- **There is no roof here to put barrels on.** This room cannot take one.

If the option does not appear at all while you hold a kit and stand inside,
the server has turned reservoirs off for that room.

## 3. Plumb a sink

Barrels on the roof do nothing until a sink below them is plumbed to them,
the same as anywhere else in the game: right click the sink and choose
**Plumb**, with a pipe wrench. A sink anywhere in the room is close enough
to a barrel.

## When the room is reset

If your room is ever reclaimed and reset for somebody else, barrels you
installed are removed with everything else you brought in. Barrels the room
came with stay.
