description=[H2]How it is different[/H2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Rooms are leased, not permanently assigned. Destroy the vehicle and the room comes back into the pool instead of leaking a slot forever
description=[*]You come out where your vehicle actually is, resolved live, not where it was up to a minute ago
description=[*]Loot stored inside adds weight to the vehicle, so the interior is not a bottomless boot
description=[*]Zombies near the vehicle accumulate while you are inside, so waiting out the night costs you something
description=[*]Every constraint is a sandbox option. Turn any of them off if it is taking away the phun
description=[/list]
description=
description=[H2]For mod authors[/H2]
description=Rooms and the vehicles that reach them are registered, not hard coded. Hook the vanilla OnInitGlobalModData event and call PhunInteriors.registerRoom and PhunInteriors.registerVehicles to add your own interiors for your own vehicles, or registerObjects to hang one off a placed item like a tent. Bindings name ordinary game script names, so you never need an id of ours. Ids are namespaced so nothing collides, and bindings are unioned rather than overridden, so adding rooms for a vehicle somebody else already covers takes nothing away from them.
