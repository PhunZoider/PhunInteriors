description=[h2]The big four[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Every vehicle gets a room that fits it.[/b] The mail van opens onto a post room, the ambulance onto a medical bay, the SWAT van onto an armory, the cattle trailer onto a barn. Furnished, with loot to match. Not one shared box with a different label on it.
description=[*][b]Walk in, walk out.[/b] Enter at the back of a van or the door of a caravan, and leave by walking out of the room. Which way you walk out decides where you land: out the back puts you at the tailgate, out the front puts you straight into a free seat in the cab. You can even climb into the back of a moving van and step back out into a free seat.
description=[*][b]Rooms never run out.[/b] A room belongs to the vehicle (or object) that opened it and keeps what you leave there. Only when every room is taken can one left unused for two weeks be reset for somebody else, and its owner is told. Claim it as a safehouse and it is never reset. Server owners pick the threshold, all the way up to "never".
description=[*][b]The room is part of the vehicle.[/b] What you store adds to the vehicle's weight. The lights run off the vehicle battery through a real generator, so a flat battery means a dark room. Tents are lit by a generator you park beside them. It is a room, not free storage.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Also in the box[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No cheesing.[/b] You cannot climb in with zombies close or from a moving vehicle. And Zeds can gather outside while you wait in there.
description=[*][b]Coming out is survivable.[/b] The ground around you is cleared as you land. The crowd is not removed; you just get a moment to see it.
description=[*][b]Contained, properly.[/b] The server keeps you in your room, so no clipping through walls, no roof walks and no wandering into the neighbour's. Walls can (optionally) be made unbreakable and fires inside are put out.
description=[*][b]Rooms stay the way they were built.[/b] A reset room is put back as built, working light switches included.
description=[*][b]Rain water.[/b] Craft a Rain Reservoir Kit and put barrels on your room's roof for a plumbed sink.
description=[*][b]Put a room behind anything.[/b] Admins can map any vehicle or placed object to any room in game, no restart. A toilet that is secretly a portkey to a workshop? Easy! See [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/remapping.md]how to map a vehicle or object to a room[/url].
description=[/list]

description=[h2]For server owners[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Set AntiCheatSpeed=4 in your server .ini[/b] or the game kicks players for every trip in or out.
description=[*]Every constraint is a sandbox option: entry time, zombie gathering, the weight factor, battery drain, how long a room is protected, unbreakable walls. Turn off whatever is taking the phun out of it.
description=[*]An in-game room window for admins: see every room and slot, port into any of them, reset or scrub one, open or close a room, and remap vehicles and edit rooms live. Changes save to PhunInteriors.json as a patch, so mod updates still reach everything you did not change.
description=[*]No required mods. PhunServer2 adds an /interiors chat command if you have it.
description=[*][b]Claim Support[/b] Built in support for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2957935793]Another Vehicle Claim System[/url].
description=[/list]

description=[h2]For mod authors[/h2]
description=Rooms and bindings are registered, not hard coded. Bindings name ordinary game script names, so neither mod depends on the other, and they add to each other rather than replacing.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Vehicle mods:[/b] one of our 83 rooms in about ten lines, no dependency. [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/vehicle-mods.md]Giving your vehicles a room[/url]
description=[*][b]Map mods:[/b] draw your own rooms on your own cells and register them. [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/map-authors.md]Adding rooms from your map[/url]
description=[*][b]Everything else:[/b] hub rooms, and putting a player in a room directly. [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/modding.md]Adding rooms and vehicles[/url]
