title=PhunInteriors
description=Instanced interiors for vehicles (and more!) that can recycle their space, contain the player, and cost something to use.

description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]990 furnished rooms in 83 designs[/b] for vans, step vans, ambulances, buses, trucks, semi trailers, caravans, RVs, shipping containers and tents, with loot to match. Vehicles from several popular vehicle mods are covered too, and only get a room when that mod is installed.
description=[*][b]Walk in, walk out.[/b] Board at the back of a van or the door of a caravan. Leave by walking out of the room. Which way you walk out decides which side of the vehicle you land on, and rooms with a cab can put you straight into a free seat.
description=[*][b]Rooms are recycled, not hoarded.[/b] A room belongs to the vehicle (or object) that opened it, and keeps whatever you leave there for as long as it is used. Leave it unused for two weeks and it can be reset and handed to another vehicle, but only once every other room is taken. You are told if it happens. Claim it as a safehouse and it is never reset.
description=[*][b]It costs something.[/b] What you store (can) add to the weight of the vehicle. The lights run off the vehicle battery or nearby generators, so a flat battery means a dark room. A few zombies gather outside while you wait in there.
description=[*][b]Coming out is survivable.[/b] You cannot see what you are stepping into, so the ground around you is cleared as you land. The crowd is not removed; you just get a moment to see it.
description=[*][b]No escape hatch.[/b] You cannot climb in with zombies close, from a moving vehicle, or from behind the wheel.
description=[*][b]Rain water.[/b] Craft a Rain Reservoir Kit and put barrels on your room's roof for a plumbed sink.
description=[*][b]Tents[/b] open onto a room too, lit by a generator you park beside them.
description=[*][b]Put a room behind anything.[/b] An admin can map (or re-map) any vehicle or placed object to any room from the in-game editor, with no restart. Want a toilet that is secretly a portkey to a workshop? Easy! See [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/remapping.md]how to map a vehicle or object to a room[/url].
description=[*][b]Walls that hold.[/b] Room walls cannot (optionally) be broken and fires inside are put out, unless the server says otherwise.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer first.[/b] One code path for single player and multiplayer, tested on a real dedicated server, including towing and passengers climbing into the back of a moving van.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]For server owners[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Every constraint is a sandbox option: entry time, zombie gathering, the weight factor, battery drain, how long a room is kept, unbreakable walls. Turn off whatever is taking the phun out of it.
description=[*]An in-game room window for admins: see every room and slot, port into any of them, reset or scrub one, and edit rooms and bindings live. Changes are saved to PhunInteriors.json when you press Save.
description=[*]No required mods. PhunServer2 adds an /interiors chat command if you have it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]For mod authors[/h2]
description=Rooms and the vehicles that reach them are registered, not hard coded. Hook the vanilla OnInitGlobalModData event and call PhunInteriors.registerRoom and PhunInteriors.registerVehicles to add interiors for your own vehicles, or registerObjects to put one behind a placed object. Bindings name ordinary game script names, so you never need an id of ours, and they add to each other rather than replacing, so covering a vehicle somebody else already covers takes nothing away from them.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Vehicle mods:[/b] give your vehicles one of our 83 rooms in about ten lines, with no map work and no dependency. [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/vehicle-mods.md]Giving your vehicles a room[/url]
description=[*][b]Map mods:[/b] draw your own rooms on your own cells and register them. [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/map-authors.md]Adding rooms from your map[/url]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Build 42 only. The mod ships its own map block (cells 87,46 to 91,48 and 89,49 to 91,49); another map mod using those cells will conflict. Server zombie distribution should not be set to Uniform.
description=
description=Running [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3790153201]Whennyago Initiative[/url]? PhunInteriors includes a performance patch for it: that mod's solar and generator upkeep is done by the server from the loaded vehicle list, rather than by every client scanning the squares around them every in-game minute. It turns itself off if Whennyago changes in a way it does not recognise, and there is a sandbox option to turn it off yourself.
