title=PhunInteriors: Interiors for RVs, Campers, Tents and more
description=Step into the back of your van, RV, camper, box truck or tent and find a real furnished room inside. 89 themed interiors across 194 vehicles, for Build 42 single player and multiplayer.
description=
description=A ground-up Build 42 RV interior mod: walk out the back to the tailgate, out the front into the driver's seat.
description=
description=[h2]The big four[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Every vehicle gets a room that fits it.[/b] The mail van opens onto a post room, the ambulance onto a medical bay, the SWAT van onto an armory, the cattle trailer onto a barn. Furnished, with matching loot.
description=[*][b]Walk in, walk out.[/b] Enter at the back of a van or the door of a caravan, and leave by walking out of the room. Out the back lands you at the tailgate, out the front in a free seat in the cab, even while the van is moving.
description=[*][b]Rooms never run out.[/b] A room belongs to the vehicle that opened it and keeps what you leave there. Only when every room is taken can one unused for two weeks be reset for somebody else, and its owner is told. A room claimed as a safehouse is never reset.
description=[*][b]The room is part of the vehicle.[/b] What you store adds to its weight. The lights run off the vehicle battery through a real generator, so a flat battery means a dark room; a trailer runs off whatever is towing it. Tents are lit by a generator you park beside them.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Also in the box[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No cheesing.[/b] No climbing in with zombies close or from a moving vehicle, and zeds gather outside while you wait.
description=[*][b]Coming out is survivable.[/b] The ground is cleared as you land. The crowd is not removed; you just get a moment to see it.
description=[*][b]Contained, properly.[/b] The server keeps you in your room: no clipping, no roof walks, no wandering next door. Walls can be made unbreakable and fires inside are put out.
description=[*][b]Rain water.[/b] Craft a Rain Reservoir Kit and put barrels on the roof for a plumbed sink.
description=[*][b]Put a room behind anything.[/b] Admins can map any vehicle or placed object to any room in game, no restart. A toilet that is secretly a portkey to a workshop? Easy! See [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunInteriors/blob/main/Docs/remapping.md]mapping a vehicle or object to a room[/url].
description=[/list]

description=[h2]For server owners[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Set AntiCheatSpeed=4 in your server .ini[/b] or the game kicks players for every trip in or out.
description=[*]Add phuninteriors to the Map setting, before Muldraugh: [b]Map=phuninteriors;Muldraugh, KY[/b]
description=[*]Every constraint is a sandbox option: entry time, zombie gathering, weight, battery drain, how long a room is protected, unbreakable walls.
description=[*]An in-game room window for admins: port into any room, reset or scrub it, open or close it, and remap vehicles and edit rooms live. Edits save to PhunInteriors.json as a patch, so mod updates still reach the rest.
description=[*]No required mods. PhunServer2 adds an /interiors chat command, and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2957935793]Another Vehicle Claim System[/url] claims are respected.
description=[/list]
